**Numbers** (reports/005/tables.md):
- **Compartment set P:** 15 MBONs with ≥ 20 synapses from right PPL1 DANs; 14 have KC input.
- **Real − shuffle wiring (real grouping, sparse):** M1 +93 [59, 127], M2 −0.124 [−0.159, −0.090].
- **Ratio readout:** M1 +41 [4, 86], M2 −0.032 [−0.042, −0.021].
- **Real − random grouping (real wiring):** M2 −0.006 [−0.029, 0.018] (sparse), +0.018
  [−0.002, 0.037] (literal). M1 is censored for all real-wiring runs.
- **Exploratory, real − random grouping (shuffle wiring, sparse):** M1 −81 [−115, −48], M2 +0.082
  [0.050, 0.114]. P's mean KC in-degree (279) is above 98.5% of random groups.

**Meaning.**
- Compartment structure does not rescue real wiring.
- The connectome-derived grouping does not beat a random one with real wiring. Its benefit with
  shuffled wiring looks like a degree effect.
- The primary readout was noisy (equal MBON weights), which put M1 at the cap.

**Next.**
- Degree-matched random grouping.
- Structured odours.
- A better compartment readout, as a new plan.
