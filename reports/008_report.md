# Report: does it matter which PNs get the high out-degree? (experiment 008)

2026-11-06. Numbers come from `reports/008/tables.md` (generated by `experiments/008/run.py`) and
`experiments/008/results/explore.txt`. Mean difference [95% bootstrap CI], 30 seeds, paired by seed.
Plan: `experiments/008/PLAN.md`, committed before the run. The four 007 conditions were rerun and
match 007 exactly (480 runs, max |ΔM2| = 0).

## Setup
Only PN→KC rewired, KC→MBON real. New: `pn_out_perm`, `shuffle_perm`, `real_perm` = the `pn_out`,
`shuffle`, `real` matrices with PN rows randomly permuted (same degree values, PN ↔ degree pairing
broken). Primary metric M2 = AUC at σ = 1, ratio readout.

## Answers (M2_ratio)

| contrast | DoOR, sparse | DoOR, literal | random odours, sparse | random odours, literal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 pn_out − pn_out_perm | **+0.052 [0.035, 0.072]** | **+0.047 [0.029, 0.068]** | +0.010 [−0.005, 0.026] | +0.015 [−0.006, 0.035] |
| Q2 pn_out_perm − random | −0.027 [−0.045, −0.009] | −0.004 [−0.019, 0.011] | −0.043 [−0.059, −0.028] | −0.051 [−0.068, −0.034] |
| Q3 shuffle − shuffle_perm | **+0.067 [0.044, 0.091]** | **+0.041 [0.018, 0.068]** | +0.010 [−0.009, 0.028] | +0.009 [−0.011, 0.028] |
| Q4 real_perm − real | −0.054 [−0.077, −0.030] | −0.021 [−0.039, −0.003] | −0.015 [−0.036, 0.006] | −0.007 [−0.033, 0.022] |

- **Q1, Q3: yes.** With DoOR odours, the out-degree benefit needs the real PN ↔ degree pairing. The same
  degree values on random PNs lose it, with or without KC in-degree kept.
- **Q2: skew alone does not help.** It is neutral or harmful with DoOR odours, and harmful with random
  odours. So the 007 crossover is: uneven coverage costs, unless it lands on the right PNs.
- **Q4:** scrambling PN identities on the real matrix hurts with DoOR odours, so the real PN assignment
  helps relative to a scrambled one. Real is still below the unscrambled shuffle (ref real − shuffle:
  −0.014 [−0.025, −0.004] sparse, −0.021 [−0.033, −0.009] literal).
- **Random odours (reference):** Q1, Q3, Q4 include 0, as predicted, since no PN is more informative there.
- The pre-stated prediction (Q1 > 0, Q3 > 0, Q2 ≈ 0 or < 0) held.

## Caveat (exploratory, not pre-registered)
54 of 149 PNs get no DoOR input (multiglomerular PNs, and glomeruli without DoOR data, set to 0).
- The real wiring puts 82.2% of KC edges on the 95 PNs that do get input.
- Row-permuted wirings put 63.7% there [55.0, 71.9].

So part, maybe most, of the Q1 effect may come from permuted wirings wasting edges on silent PNs. That is
partly a DoOR coverage effect, not only "more coverage for more informative glomeruli". It does not
separate the two.

## What this means
- The positive connectome effect from 007 is specific to *which* PNs have the high out-degree; it is not
  a generic property of a skewed distribution.
- It is still a degree-level effect. Specific real PN→KC partners never beat the shuffle (001–008).
- Limits: one odour dataset with incomplete coverage; ratio readout only; many contrasts.

## Next
Permute out-degree only among the 95 DoOR-input PNs (and, separately, only among silent PNs). If
pn_out still beats that control, informative-channel matching holds within measured glomeruli; if not,
the effect is mostly about silent PNs.
