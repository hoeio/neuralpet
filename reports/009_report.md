- **Q1: yes.** Moving the out-degree values only among the 95 PNs that receive DoOR input still costs
  robustness. So matching high KC coverage to the more informative measured channels helps, not only
  keeping edges off silent PNs. It is about 40% (sparse) and 65% (literal) of the 008 effect; the rest
  is consistent with edges moved onto silent PNs. Effect sizes are moderate (d_z 0.44, 0.53), and the
  sparse CI lower bound is close to 0.
- **Q2: ≈ 0, as predicted.** Permuting silent PNs' rows does nothing, which is the expected sanity result.
- pn_out_perm_live is no better than random wiring on M2 (sparse +0.003 [−0.009, 0.016], literal
  +0.012 [−0.002, 0.026]).

## What this means
Across 007–009 there is one connectome-derived effect: with real (DoOR) odours, the FlyWire PN out-degrees
help noise robustness, and they help only when they sit on the PNs they have in the connectome. That
includes matching within measured glomeruli. Specific KC partners still never beat a degree-preserving
shuffle. All of this is in our simple binary model with designed adapters, with one odour dataset.
