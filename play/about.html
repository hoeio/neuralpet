About NeuralPet

What it is. A bio-inspired computer simulation. It is not alive and it is not conscious, and it is not a faithful emulation of any animal. The pet's "brain" is a simplified model of the mushroom body, a learning centre of the fruit-fly brain. Its wiring is copied from a reconstructed fly brain. Everything else is our own design: the worm-like body, how it moves, the senses, the energy rules, and how the brain is connected to the body. Moods and words on the page are labels for numbers in the model. They do not mean the pet feels anything.

How teaching works. When you praise or scold, the model gets a short dopamine-like signal. That signal changes the connection strengths of the brain cells that are active at that moment. The cells that are active depend on the smell the pet senses. Over time this changes how the pet steers toward a smell or away from it.

Research. The research behind this, including the negative results, is in the project report (reports/FINAL.md). The play app runs on frozen research code and is never used as research evidence.
